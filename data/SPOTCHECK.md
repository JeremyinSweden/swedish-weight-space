## Reference paragraphs

- **ref_test_003**
  - sv: Fjällen i norra Sverige lockar vandrare från hela världen. Kungsleden sträcker sig över fyrtio mil mellan Abisko och Hemavan. Längs leden finns stugor där man kan övernatta.
  - en: The mountains of northern Sweden attract hikers from all over the world. The King's Trail stretches over four hundred kilometres between Abisko and Hemavan. Along the trail there are huts where you can stay overnight.
- **ref_test_005**

  - en: The right of public access gives everyone the right to spend time in nature, even on land that someone else owns. You may pick berries and mushrooms and camp for one night. At the same time, you must show consideration for animals, nature and landowners.
